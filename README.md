Strip even parity and base-10 zeroes prior to handling larger prime candidates:
```javascript

  factors.push(2n, 5n);
  n /= 10n;
```

### Strategy 2: Sum of Digits & Modulo 9
Apply digital root checks to instantly pull out multiples of 3 and 9:
$$\text{If } \sum d_i \equiv 0 \pmod 9 \implies 9 \mid N$$

### Strategy 3: Difference of Squares (Fermat Method)
Target close square pairs for composite numbers near perfect roots:
$$N = a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$$
*Example:* $899 = 30^2 - 1^2 = (30 - 1)(30 + 1) = 29 \times 31$
